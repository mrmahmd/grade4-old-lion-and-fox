The Blend Lab

1 · Read and learn

When you blend, you join sounds together to read a word. In a consonant blend you can hear both consonant sounds. Slide them together without adding an extra vowel.

Teacher cue · Point to each sound. Model the blend and the whole word. Have the class echo, then let partners take turns.
